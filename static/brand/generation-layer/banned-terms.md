# Built for Exit — Banned Terms

Honor these on every generated asset. They keep the brand on the PE × luxury-auto × elite-OS line and off the SaaS-guru line.

## Visual — never

- gradients, color blends, glossy 3D, chrome, bevels
- drop shadows or any shadow (the brand ships **no shadows, ever**)
- neon, glow, bloom, lens flare, cyberpunk
- filled or 3D icons; cartoon icons; any illustration or drawn figure work
- stock-photo smiling teams; conference-room photography; handshakes
- any second saturated color competing with Exit Yellow
- Exit Yellow covering more than a small fraction of the frame
- circuit boards, glowing AI brains, robots, sci-fi UI clichés
- rounded "friendly startup" blobs; playful mascots

## Voice — never

- unlock, supercharge, synergy, leverage (as filler), revolutionary, game-changer, paradigm, disrupt
- "transformational operational excellence" and any buzzword stack
- "10x your business", hustle / grind / motivational-poster cadence
- SaaS-landing cadence ("the all-in-one platform for…")
- consultant / agency throat-clearing
- AI hype OR AI doom framing
- em dashes

## Always

- dark canonical ground; flat matte finish; 1px steel borders
- Exit Yellow at exactly one value moment
- numbers in IBM Plex Mono; headlines uppercase grotesk
- photography is founder-alone / industrial / B&W
- stamp the real wordmark on finished assets; never let the model draw the wordmark from scratch
