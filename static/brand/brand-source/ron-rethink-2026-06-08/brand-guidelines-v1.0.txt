Brand Positioning
Category
Business Transformation
Subcategory
Transferability & Exit Readiness
Core Idea
Transferability Creates Freedom.
Mission
Transform founder-dependent businesses into transferable assets.
Definition
A transferable business is a business that can be handed to another person and continue operating successfully.
Not just a buyer.
A successor.
A CEO.
A management team.
A family member.
Or your future self after 90 days away.
