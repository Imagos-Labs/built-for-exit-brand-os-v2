# Logo matrix

The signature is **the Hollow 4** — a bold, slightly oblique outlined numeral 4 in Exit Yellow, the "for" in B4E (Built **4** Exit). It works alone at small sizes, as the favicon, as a watermark, on merch. Never overuse it. The wordmark is heavy oblique grotesk, all caps. Every asset here was generated with gpt-image-2 from Ron's brand-sheet logo system as the reference.

## Rules

- **Mark alone** at small sizes and as the app/social icon. **Horizontal lockup** in navs. **Monogram** for compact contexts and merch.
- The wordmark is **all caps**, heavy oblique grotesk. The mark is **always Exit Yellow on dark**.
- **One accent:** the 4 is the only yellow in the mark. Don't add a second.
- **Never overuse the 4.** It's a signature, not a texture.
- **Always-on wordmark:** stamp the real wordmark PNG onto finished assets — don't let an image model draw the text from scratch.
