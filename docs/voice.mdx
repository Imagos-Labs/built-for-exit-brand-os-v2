# Voice

Short. Direct. High conviction. No fluff. No buzzwords. No hype. Ask the hard question, then state the system that answers it. The reader is a serious operator — respect that.

> **Good:** Can your business survive 90 days without you?
> **Bad:** Unlock transformational operational excellence.

## The motto

<p style={{fontFamily:"'Inter Tight',sans-serif", fontWeight:800, textTransform:'uppercase', fontSize:'1.8rem', margin:'16px 0', letterSpacing:'-0.01em'}}>Transferability creates freedom.</p>

## Naming

- The brand: **Built for Exit** (wordmark all caps: BUILT FOR EXIT; monogram B4E).
- The people: **founders / operators** (and **owners**). Not "clients," not "users."
- Trademark the proprietary nouns: **Transferable™**, **The System™**, **Exit Readiness Index™ / Exit Readiness Score™**, **Built For Exit Approved™**.

## Tone

| Dial | Setting |
|------|---------|
| Posture | engineered, deliberate, institutional |
| Energy | calm authority, never anxious, never hype |
| Formality | high-conviction and plainspoken; numbers do the talking |

## On-brand lines

- "Can your business survive 90 days without you?"
- "Your business should work. Without you."
- "Most founders don't own a business. They own a job with overhead."
- "Exit is not the goal. Optionality is."
- "If it's not documented, it's not transferable."
- "Freedom is a system."

## Off-brand lines (don't)

- "Unlock transformational operational excellence." (buzzword stack)
- startup-guru / hustle / motivational cadence.
- SaaS-landing cadence ("the all-in-one platform for...").
- AI hype OR AI doom framing.

## Banned vocabulary

**Words:** unlock, supercharge, synergy, revolutionary, game-changer, paradigm, disrupt, "transformational excellence", "10x your business", "leverage" as filler, world-class, cutting-edge. **No em dashes.**

**Visual (for image models):** gradients, drop shadows, glossy 3D, neon/glow, filled or 3D icons, any illustration, smiling-team or conference-room stock photos, any second saturated color competing with Exit Yellow. Full list: [`banned-terms.md`](/brand/generation-layer/banned-terms.md).

:::note[The one rule under everything]
If the logo were removed, the page should still read as *a private equity firm, a luxury automotive brand, and an elite operating-system company had a child.* If a line sounds like a coach or a SaaS landing page, cut it.
:::
