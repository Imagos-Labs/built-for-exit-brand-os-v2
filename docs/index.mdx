## What Built for Exit is

Built for Exit transforms founder-dependent businesses into transferable assets — companies that can be handed to a successor, a CEO, a family member, a buyer, or your future self after a month away, and keep running. Most founders think "exit" means selling. It doesn't. Selling is one form of transfer. The objective is optionality.

- **Core idea:** *Transferability creates freedom.*
- **Mission:** transform founder-dependent businesses into transferable assets.
- **Thesis:** most founders don't own a business — they own a job with overhead. AI finally made it affordable to document, systemize, de-risk, and transfer a company in months.
- **Archetype:** Architect (structured, systematic, engineered) + Operator (practical, outcomes-driven).
- **Founders:** Gary Sheng & Ron Roberts II. HQ: Dallas.

## The success test

Hand only this repo to an agent with no designer and ask for a landing page, a deck, and a social set. If the three come back unmistakably one brand — dark, engineered, Exit-Yellow at the value moments, the Hollow 4 quietly present — the OS is real.

## How to consume it

- **Source of truth** is the repo root: `BRAND.md` (read first) + `brand.json` + `tokens.css`.
- The **asset package** lives under `static/brand/` (served at `/brand/...`).
- This portal is the **human gallery** rendered over that package.
