## Soul (Phase A — from Ron's docs, exact language preserved)

- **Core idea:** "Transferability Creates Freedom."
- **Mission:** Transform founder-dependent businesses into transferable assets.
- **Definition (Transferable™):** "A transferable business is a business that can be handed to another person and continue operating successfully." Not just a buyer — a successor, a CEO, a management team, a family member, a partner, or your future self after a month away.
- **The feeling:** Engineered. Deliberate. Institutional. Calm authority, not hype. "Everything should feel engineered. Not playful."
- **Anti-position:** Do NOT feel like a startup guru, business coach, consultant, marketing agency, or SaaS startup.
- **Should feel like:** Porsche, Blackstone, Anduril, Palantir, Linear, Stripe Press.
- **Archetype:** Primary — **Architect** (structured, systematic, deliberate, engineered). Secondary — **Operator** (practical, execution-focused, outcomes-driven).
- **Name for people served:** founders / operators.
- **Non-negotiable element:** The Hollow 4. Never overused.
- **100-year mark / final rule:** "If someone removed the logo from the page, the design should still feel like a private equity firm, a luxury automotive brand, and an elite operating system company had a child."
- **Motto / taglines:** "Transferability Creates Freedom." · "Freedom is a system." · "Exit is not the goal. Optionality is." · "Your business should work. Without you."

## Voice (Phase A cont.)

Short. Direct. High conviction. No fluff. No buzzwords. No hype.
- Good: "Can your business survive 90 days without you?"
- Bad: "Unlock transformational operational excellence."
- Trademark the proprietary nouns: **Transferable™**, **The System™**, **Exit Readiness Index™ / Exit Readiness Score™**, **Built For Exit Approved™**.

## Executable decisions (Phase B — LOCKED)

13. **Palette.** Ground `#050505` (Primary Black) · `#0F0F10` (Carbon, secondary dark) · `#1A1A1D` (Graphite, cards) · `#2D2D31` (Steel, borders) · `#F3F3F3` (Soft White, primary text) · `#9A9A9F` (Muted Gray, secondary copy). Accent **Exit Yellow `#DFFF00`** (hover `#C6E900`) — used sparingly, value moments only (<~8% of a surface). Light-surface inversion exists (white bg / black text / yellow accent — see invoice mockups) but dark is dominant and canonical.
14. **Type.** Display **Neue Haas Grotesk Display** (Bold, Black) → alt Helvetica Now Display → fallback **Inter Tight**. Body **Inter** (Regular, Medium). Mono **IBM Plex Mono** for scores / valuation metrics / statistics / dashboards. Headline rules: uppercase, bold, tight line-height, large scale.
15. **Motifs.** (1) **The Hollow 4** signature mark. (2) **Dependency diagram** — founder at center, everything connected. (3) **Before → After transformation** table. (4) **Monochrome scorecard** (`72 / 100`) + five-pillar **exit-readiness radar**. (5) Progression rail: DEPENDENT → DOCUMENTED → SYSTEMIZED → TRANSFERABLE → EXIT READY.
16. **Aesthetic direction.** Dark, engineered, institutional. Generous whitespace, never cramped. 1px steel borders, NO shadows ever. Outlined / geometric / minimal icons only — no gradients, no filled icons, no illustrations. Photography: founder alone, airports, private hangars, empty office, documentation, process maps, manufacturing, warehouses, control rooms; B&W or desaturated. Avoid smiling teams, stock photos, conference rooms. Motion: fade / reveal / connect / count-up. No bouncing, elastic, or flashy motion.
17. **Banned vocabulary / visual bans.** See `static/brand/generation-layer/banned-terms.md`. Visual: no gradients, no drop shadows, no filled/3D icons, no illustration, no stock-photo smiling teams. Voice: no buzzwords/hype (no "unlock", "transformational excellence", "synergy", "10x your business", guru-speak).
18. **Logo concept.** Primary wordmark **BUILT FOR EXIT** in heavy grotesk (stacked two-line lockup or single-line). Signature mark **The Hollow 4** (negative-space numeral 4, the "for" in B4E). Monogram **B4E**. Variants: Exit-Yellow-on-black, white-on-black (mono), black-on-white (knockout/light). Used as watermark, favicon, icon, footer element, merch. Never overused.
19. **Production form.** Full brand-os-wiki (Docusaurus + brand.txt), cloned from the v1 scaffold next door.
20. **Home + deploy.** In place at `imagos-HQ/03-built-for-exit/brand-v2/`. Own git repo (TBD). Portal deploy to Vercel = optional follow-on. Live landing-page rebuild = separate follow-on task (out of scope this run).
21. **Reference set (should-feel-like, for the image model):** Linear (typography, spacing, minimalism), Anduril (authority, defense-grade confidence), Porsche (premium execution), Stripe Press (editorial sophistication), Blackstone (institutional trust), Palantir.

## Structural canon (from the website-copy doc)

- **Five Levers / The System™:** 01 Documentation · 02 Systems · 03 Operations · 04 Revenue · 05 Financial & Diligence.
- **Progression:** DEPENDENT → DOCUMENTED → SYSTEMIZED → TRANSFERABLE → EXIT READY.
- **Engagement timeline:** ASSESS → DOCUMENT → SYSTEMIZE → DE-RISK → OPTIMIZE → EXIT READY.
- **Transferability creates options:** Succession · Management Transition · Scale · Exit.
- **Key stats:** 50% of business exits are involuntary (the 5 Ds: Death, Disability, Divorce, Disagreement, Distress). 80% of owners have no transition plan. 3x–4x → 7x–8x EBITDA at exit.
- **Footer canon:** Dallas HQ · Money Back Guarantee · Navigation · Social.
